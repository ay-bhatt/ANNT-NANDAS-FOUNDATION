Our Journey
 Everygreat movement begins with a single step.
Our journey began on 1st May 2023 in the small Himalayan village of Mundoli.
There was no grand infrastructure.
No sports academy.
No large funding.
No expensive equipment.
Only one vision.
To ensure that no talented child remains unseen simply because they were born in a remote  
village.
With only two bicycles and twelve enthusiastic children, our founder, Mr. Kalam Singh Bisht,  
an Ex-Serviceman, International Ultra Trail Runner, social worker, and passionate mentor,  
began conducting daily training sessions.
These sessions were never limited to sports.
Every morning became a classroom of discipline.
Every run became a lesson in determination.
Every conversation became an opportunity to inspire confidence.
The Foundation slowly became a second home where children learned not only how to run  
faster or cycle farther but also how to dream bigger, think independently, respect others,  
protect nature, preserve their culture, and believe in themselves.
As word spread from one village to another, more families began trusting the Foundation.
More children joined.
More volunteers stepped forward.
What began as a local initiative soon became a growing movement for social transformation  
across the Himalayan region.
Today, ANNT NANDAS FOUNDATION continues that same journey with the same values that  
inspired its beginning—service, discipline, integrity, compassion, and unwavering  
commitment to the community.

1. What is ANNT NANDAS FOUNDATION?
ANNT NANDAS FOUNDATION is a non-profit organization established on 1st May 2023 in
Mundoli, Chamoli, Uttarakhand. Our mission is to empower underprivileged children and  
rural communities through sports, education, skill development, healthcare, environmental
conservation, livelihood support, and community development.
We work to discover hidden talent, nurture it through guidance and training, and create
opportunities that help individuals become confident, independent, and responsible  
citizens.
2. Why was the Foundation established?
The Foundation was established after recognising that many talented children in remote
Himalayan villages lacked access to opportunities, guidance, quality education, sports
infrastructure, and career support.
Our founder believed that poverty should never prevent a child from achieving their  
dreams.
ANNT NANDAS FOUNDATION was created to bridge this gap and help children discover their
potential .
3. Why is sports the Foundation's main focus?
Sports teach much more than physical fitness.
They develop discipline, confidence, leadership, teamwork, resilience, and determination.
Sports also create opportunities for education, scholarships, government careers, and
national and international representation.
For us, sports are a powerful tool for social transformation.
4. What is the ANNT Talent Discovery Model?
The ANNT Talent Discovery Model is our unique community-based approach to identifying
hidden talent.
Instead of waiting for children to come to us, our team visits villages, interacts with families,
counsels children, observes their interests and abilities, provides training, and continues
mentoring them until they become confident and independent individuals.
